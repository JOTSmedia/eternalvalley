## 1. STRATEGIC POINT OF VIEW: "THE ENDOWED ARCHITECTURE"

The digital memorial space is plagued by web 2.0 slop—grief commodified into social media feeds, rotating banners, and $4.99/mo subscription models that feel like renting a grave. Eternal Valley wins by rejecting "social media for the dead." 

**The Two-Door Strategy:**
The current codebase exhibits a dual-identity: a highly accessible, charity-driven Pet Memorial ("Somewhere Over the Rainbow Bridge") and a highly premium, architectural human legacy pitch ("Memory is a Place"). 
This is not a conflict; this is the funnel.
- **DOOR 1 (The Trojan Horse):** The Pet Memorial. Highly viral, emotionally safe, lower barrier to entry. We partner with vets. We map the earth. It trains the market that *digital space is sacred*.
- **DOOR 2 (The Apex Offering):** The Human Legacy. Pure luxury. Omni-sensory audio. Endowed permanence. No feeds. No subscriptions. 

We do not offer profiles; we offer *places*. We do not sell subscriptions; we sell *endowments*. 

## 2. NAMED AESTHETIC: "BRUTALIST TRANSCENDENCE"

- **Visuals:** Dark mode default. Architectural brutalism (stone, iron, monolithic geometry) intersected with hyper-organic, ephemeral elements (glowing rainbows, blooming seasonal cherry blossoms, live weather logic).
- **UI Paradigm:** High contrast, zero friction. The UI is a lens, not a dashboard. No pop-ups, no gamification, no "notification badges" on grief. 
- **Typography:** Stark serifs mixed with ultra-minimalist monospace for systemic data (coordinates, timestamps). 

## 3. CORE BRAND RULES

1. **NEVER use:** "profile", "feed", "user", "post", "social network", "account".
